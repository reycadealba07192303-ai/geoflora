Label review: Lantana camara (250 images)

How to review
  • Click a photo card (or press Tab) and press A = approved, R = rejected, U = uncertain, C = clear. Focus jumps to the next photo. Click a photo to open it full-size.
  • Approve only when the photo clearly shows this species. Reject when it is a different plant, not a plant, or the plant is not identifiable (for example a map, herbarium label only, or a blurry patch). Use uncertain when you cannot tell: uncertain images are not used for training.
  • The GBIF record and source links show the original observation and its community identification.
  • Decisions are saved in this browser automatically. Close the page at any time and continue later.
  • When done, click Download CSV. It saves GF-SP-0002_Lantana_camara.csv (usually in Downloads). Then run:
    python scripts/review_images.py import "<path to downloaded GF-SP-0002_Lantana_camara.csv>" --reviewer "Your Full Name"
  • Condition tags (lighting, background, organ, growth stage) are optional but help the robustness analysis.
