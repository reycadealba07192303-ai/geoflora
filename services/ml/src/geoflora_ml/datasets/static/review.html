Review checklist and decisions

Inspect the full image and source observation. Approve only when visible evidence supports the target species. Record the evidence and your name. Check source location, license and attribution; missing GPS does not establish country.

Flag ambiguous images or unresolved provenance for specialist review. Reject incorrect, unusable or out-of-scope images with a reason. Relabel only when the corrected target species is verified: relabelling makes the record eligible for export. Near duplicates must stay in one future split; similarity alone does not verify a label.
